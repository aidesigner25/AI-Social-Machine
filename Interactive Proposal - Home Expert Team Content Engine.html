How much of my time does this take?

A short cloning session at the start, a pasted link for each listing, and time to approve or revise each batch. Everything else is produced and published by the system.

Will the content sound like me?

Copy and video are tied to each member's likeness, tone, humor and engagement strategy. Nothing is posted until you approve it, and anything that misses is sent back with your feedback.

Which platforms does it post to?

Facebook, Instagram and YouTube Shorts as standard. TikTok and LinkedIn are supported if you want them.

Can we post more than eight times a week?

Yes. Eight is the baseline and the total can be raised on request. Running costs may rise with heavier output, since they follow usage.

How is MLS data handled?

Property details, images and remarks are extracted as allowed by your MLS provider's API terms. Listing posts start from a link you paste.

What happens if something breaks?

The first 14 days after deployment include support for defects and production errors, and we guarantee the system runs as outlined here. After that, fixes and changes are handled case by case, and ongoing maintenance is optional.
